Cheryl Spencer-Morley

Founder & Principal, Strategic Communications and Crisis Management

Book a 15-minute call↗

Cheryl Spencer-Morley brings more than 25 years of senior-level communications experience across federal, provincial, and municipal government in Canada. She leads the crisis communications practice at Spencer Morley Consulting, and is the primary client contact for all crisis, media relations, and communications counsel engagements.

She has guided organizations through some of the highest-stakes communications challenges in the public sector \u2014 budget controversies, housing disputes, wildfire responses, public health emergencies, policing incidents, and the kind of scrutiny that defines reputations for years. Her approach is direct: the next 24 hours define your organization\u2019s legacy. She plans for that before the crisis arrives.

Her crisis management practice is built on frontline experience, not theory. Cheryl has been in the room when decisions had to be made under pressure \u2014 advising councils, executives, and senior officials who could not afford to be wrong. That experience shapes every engagement, from crisis plan development to real-time response support.

Her media relations training is equally grounded in practice. She coaches spokespeople \u2014 elected officials, executives, administrators \u2014 to face cameras, microphones, and hostile questions with confidence and clarity. The training is built around realistic scenarios specific to each client\u2019s actual risk profile.

Cheryl is certified in emergency management and traumatic event systems. She serves governments, school boards, municipalities, and organizations across Alberta that cannot afford to be unprepared.

What Cheryl works on

Crisis Management

End-to-end crisis planning and real-time response support. Budget cuts, housing controversies, wildfires, public health emergencies, police incidents \u2014 high-stakes situations where the next 24 hours define the outcome.

Media Relations Training

Spokesperson coaching for cameras, microphones, and high-pressure public situations. Built around realistic, client-specific scenarios \u2014 not generic media training frameworks.

On-Demand Communications Counsel

Retained strategic communications support available when organizations need expert counsel \u2014 without the overhead of a full-time director.

Government Communications

Deep experience advising councils, executives, and senior officials across all three levels of Canadian government on communications strategy, public messaging, and stakeholder relations.

Emergency Communications Planning

Pre-crisis planning that ensures organizations know exactly what to say, who says it, and through which channels \u2014 before the emergency arrives.

Reputational Risk Management

Strategic counsel for organizations facing the kind of public scrutiny that shapes long-term reputations. Proactive, reactive, and recovery communications.

Fractional Public Information Officer Services

Embedded, hands-on communications capacity for a municipality managing an active crisis, a recovery period, or a staffing gap \u2014 council communications, stakeholder relations, social media, and website updates, delivered day to day rather than advised from outside.

Strategic Plan Facilitation

Facilitated strategic planning for agricultural service boards and rural economic development bodies, turning board priorities into a workable plan.

What it looked like from the inside

Cheryl walked into the hardest chapter of Jasper\u2019s history and made herself part of the community within days. She wasn\u2019t a local but you\u2019d never have known it. She showed up for residents and business owners and earned their trust when trust was hard to come by for anyone.

What I\u2019ll never forget is her steadiness. When our local leaders had to have the toughest conversations of the recovery, the ones that took real courage, Cheryl was in their corner. She coached them through it with a clear head and decades of experience. She ran virtual media conference after virtual media conference that allowed everyone involved to share crucial updates with media. As a bonus, she had a knack for noticing when local staff were stretched too thin, where she would quietly take work off their plates so they could rest instead of burning out.

Cheryl isn\u2019t just an emergency communications expert. She brings that same sharp thinking to strategic planning and everyday work. Put her in a room well before the crisis hits and she\u2019ll help you see it coming.

Janelle Lane, Information Officer, Parks Canada
