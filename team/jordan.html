Jordan Morley

AI Change Management Consultant

Book a 15-minute call↗

Jordan Morley leads the AI consulting practice at Spencer Morley Consulting, working with Canadian municipalities and small businesses across Canada to adopt artificial intelligence responsibly \u2014 without jargon, without software sales, and without the overhead of an enterprise consultant.

His focus is on the gap between where organizations are and where they need to be: staff already using AI tools without governance, privacy obligations arriving faster than policy can be written, and AI platforms that can\u2019t find or recommend businesses that haven\u2019t been optimized for them. He builds practical frameworks that close these gaps.

On the municipal side, Jordan specializes in ATIA and POPA compliance, shadow AI risk management, AI acceptable use policy development, and the Municipal AI Framework \u2014 a structured approach to readiness built specifically for small and rural municipalities that lack dedicated IT or legal resources.

On the small business side, he runs the Discoverability Index \u2014 scoring how a business appears (or fails to appear) across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Copilot against its named competitors \u2014 and designs the remediation plan that follows. He also runs the Staff AI Risk Review, the separate inside-the-business look at where staff use of AI creates privacy or quality risk. He also implements WebMCP (Model Context Protocol) configurations that make businesses findable and recommendable by AI platforms.
