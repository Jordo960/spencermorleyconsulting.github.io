Straight answers

The questions people ask before they call.

What does Spencer Morley Consulting do?

Two practices under one firm: strategic communications and crisis management for governments and institutions, and practical AI consulting for municipalities, small businesses, and non-profits. Based in Edmonton, serving all of Canada, 100% Canadian owned.

Who leads the two practices?

Cheryl Spencer-Morley leads strategic communications and crisis management, with 25+ years of senior government communications experience across federal, provincial, and municipal levels, ICS 300 (Incident Command System Canada, 2015) and Traumatic Event Systems training (Center for Trauma Informed Practices, 2021), and the lead crisis communications role in the 2024 Jasper wildfire response. Jordan Morley leads the AI practice, covering municipal privacy compliance, staff training, AI-search discoverability, and workflow automation.

Can you provide communications staff during an active crisis or a staffing gap?

Yes — that is the Fractional Public Information Officer (PIO) service. Rather than advising from outside, we embed and do the work day to day: council communications, stakeholder relations, media, social media, and website updates. Cheryl Spencer-Morley spent roughly two and a half months on the ground with the Municipality of Jasper during its 2024 wildfire response, effectively functioning as the municipality's public information officer through re-entry messaging, debris-removal coordination, housing support, and the return of the visitor economy. The arrangement suits an active incident, a recovery period, or a vacancy you can't fill quickly.

Do you work with small businesses in Edmonton, or only municipalities?

Both, as two separate practices — neither is a sideline of the other. The small-business practice serves Edmonton and Alberta owner-operated and small companies across professional services, construction and trades, accounting, and manufacturing: internal AI use and risk reviews, staff AI adoption and training, operational efficiency, and workflow automation for quoting, bookings, lead handling, customer service, and sales admin — plus AI-search visibility, so customers searching by AI actually find you. It runs on its own track with its own diagnostics: the free Discoverability Check, the free Staff AI Risk Review, and the Discoverability Report.

How do municipalities usually start?

With a compliance position check. Provincial privacy legislation such as Alberta's ATIA and POPA required a Privacy Management Program by June 11, 2026; the readiness assessment maps your exposure, shadow-AI risk, and policy gaps, and produces a roadmap council can act on.

How do small businesses usually start?

With one of two diagnostics. The free Discoverability Check tests three AI engines and shows whether they recommend your business, who they name instead, and one visible gap, emailed within a business day. The full Report covers five engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot) with a 0–100 score against named competitors. The free Staff AI Risk Review maps how AI is used inside your business: shadow-AI risk, and where AI can safely save your team time.
