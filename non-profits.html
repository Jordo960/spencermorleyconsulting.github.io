Canadian non-profits and AI

Adoption ran ahead
of governance.

Most organizations already using AI have no policy, no training plan and no clear picture of where it is saving time versus creating risk. Of the non-profits using AI, 64% have no policies and aren’t developing any.

The ones getting real value started by using AI safely, then built from there.

Source

The standard we train to

Ask. Check. Tell.

Ask whether a task is appropriate for AI in the first place. Check every output for accuracy and bias before it’s used. Tell people when AI was involved.

A shared rule simple enough for your whole team to remember is what turns scattered, unguided use into responsible use.

What happens in a readiness conversation?

We look at how your team already uses AI and where sensitive data could be exposed, map the repetitive work eating your staff’s hours, and set out the two or three moves that free up the most time and reduce the most risk first. Then we walk you through it in a 45-minute briefing, in plain language your board and staff can use. There is no cost and no obligation.

Will AI replace our staff or volunteers?

No. The goal is to remove the administrative load that keeps them from the mission. Every system is built to fit the tools you already use, sized for a lean team without an IT department, and documented so you are never dependent on us to keep it running.
