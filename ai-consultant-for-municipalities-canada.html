Common questions

Questions municipalities ask before engaging

What does a municipal AI consultant do?

A municipal AI consultant helps a local government adopt AI without breaking privacy law. That means assessing where staff already use AI, drafting an acceptable use policy that council can adopt, training staff to use AI safely with citizen data, and where appropriate deploying a private model and automating routine administrative work under human oversight.

Do small and rural municipalities need AI consulting?

Yes, and often more than large cities. Small and rural municipalities have the same legal obligations under provincial privacy law but rarely have dedicated IT or privacy staff. Shadow AI — staff using unapproved tools — is common, and a single mishandled record can become a public investigation. Consulting closes that gap at a scale and price built for smaller teams.

Does Spencer Morley Consulting work outside Alberta?

Yes. The municipal AI practice is available to municipalities in every Canadian province and territory, with guidance written to each jurisdiction's privacy law — FOIPPA in British Columbia, MFIPPA in Ontario, FIPPA in Manitoba, LAFOIP in Saskatchewan, and ATIA/POPA in Alberta. Delivery is remote and available nationwide.

What happens if our municipality missed the privacy compliance deadline?

A passed deadline does not remove the obligation — a municipality without a Privacy Management Program in place remains exposed and can face formal complaints. The first step is a readiness assessment to document current AI exposure and policy gaps, followed by a fast-tracked acceptable use policy for council adoption.

How much does municipal AI consulting cost?

Municipal engagements are quoted per scope, because a readiness assessment for a small village differs from a policy build plus private LLM deployment for a larger town. Contact Spencer Morley Consulting for a quote based on your population, staff size, and current AI exposure.
