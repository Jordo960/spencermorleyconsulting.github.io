What Is Answer Engine Optimization?
How to Earn the Featured Answer

There is a meaningful difference between ranking on the first page and being the answer the system gives. AEO closes that gap — for voice search, AI Overviews, and every platform that synthesizes a direct response instead of returning a list.

The Core Idea

What AEO Actually Means

Answer Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring your content so that AI systems, voice assistants, and search engines select your business as the direct answer to a question. Not a link in a list — the answer itself.

The distinction matters because the way search is consumed has fundamentally shifted. When someone asks Siri "who's the best plumber in Edmonton" or types "top AI consultants in Alberta" into Perplexity, they are not looking at a ranked list and choosing a result. They are receiving a synthesized response. The business that appears in that response was not placed there by an ad — it was selected because its content was structured in a way that allowed the AI to confidently extract and deliver an answer.

Google's AI Overviews now appear on more than 60% of commercial queries — and when an AI Overview is present, click-through rates on the traditional organic results below it drop by 34–59%. The featured answer is not just a nice-to-have. For many query types, it is the only result that matters.

AEO is how you earn that position — across Google, voice, and every AI platform that generates answers rather than listing links.

The Tactics

How to Optimize Your Business for AEO

  1. 1
    Implement FAQPage schema on every key pageFAQPage is the single highest-ROI schema type for AEO. It marks up question-and-answer pairs so that AI systems and search engines can extract them directly. Every service page, every location page, and your homepage should have at least 4–6 FAQs marked up with FAQPage schema. The questions should be the actual questions your customers ask — not keyword phrases.
  2. 2
    Write content that answers questions in the first sentenceAI systems extracting featured answers look for content where the answer immediately follows the question — no preamble, no throat-clearing, no marketing language before the substance. Use the question as the heading (H2 or H3). Put the direct answer in the first 40–60 words of the response. Every word of preamble is a reason for the AI to skip to a competitor's cleaner answer.
  3. 3
    Complete and maintain your Google Business ProfileVoice search and AI Overviews for local queries are heavily weighted toward GBP data. Your services list, description, hours, and photos need to be accurate, detailed, and recently updated. A GBP last touched in 2023 is a liability for AEO — it signals staleness to every system that queries it.
  4. 4
    Add LocalBusiness and Service schema to your websiteSchema.org markup gives AI systems explicit, machine-readable information about what your business is, what it does, and where it operates — rather than requiring inference from page text. LocalBusiness schema with areaServed, hasOfferCatalog, and review properties is the baseline for any Alberta service business pursuing AEO.
  5. 5
    Build review velocity across platformsFor local business queries, review signals are consistently weighted in AI answer selection. A systematic approach to requesting reviews from satisfied customers — at the point of service, via follow-up email, with a direct link — builds the velocity that AI systems interpret as current, active trust. Aim for at least 4–6 new reviews per month across Google and industry platforms.
  6. 6
    Earn third-party citations on authoritative platformsAI systems triangulate credibility from external mentions. Chamber of commerce membership listings, industry association directories, Better Business Bureau profiles, local media mentions, and supplier/partner pages all contribute to your authority signal. Each consistent mention reinforces the same entity and increases AI confidence in recommending you.

Common Questions

AEO FAQ

What is the most important schema type for AEO?

FAQPage schema is the highest-ROI single investment for most businesses. It directly feeds AI systems the question-and-answer pairs they use to generate answers, and it enables Google's People Also Ask and Featured Snippet features. After FAQPage, LocalBusiness and Service schema are the next highest priorities for Alberta service businesses.

Can I optimize for AEO myself, or do I need an expert?

The content principles — direct answers, question headings, FAQ format — are things any business owner can implement with guidance. The schema markup implementation requires technical knowledge or a specialist. The Staff AI Risk Review is the recommended starting point: it identifies specifically which queries you're missing and which AEO tactics would have the highest impact for your business category and location, so any investment is targeted rather than generic.

How does AEO relate to voice search?

Voice search is essentially AEO applied to audio output. When someone asks a voice assistant a question, it reads a single answer aloud — and that answer comes from the same featured-snippet and structured-data signals that AEO targets. Optimizing for AEO improves voice search performance automatically, because the underlying signals are the same.
