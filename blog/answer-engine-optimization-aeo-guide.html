What does AEO mean?

Answer Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring your content so that AI systems, voice assistants, and search engines select your business as the direct answer to a question.

How should your content answer a question?

AI systems extracting featured answers look for content where the answer immediately follows the question; no preamble, no throat-clearing, no marketing language before the substance. Use the question as the heading (H2 or H3). Put the direct answer in the first 40-60 words of the response. Every word of preamble is a reason for the AI to skip to a competitor's cleaner answer.

What should you do with your Google Business Profile?

Voice search and AI Overviews for local queries are heavily weighted toward GBP data. Your services list, description, hours, and photos need to be accurate, detailed, and recently updated. A GBP last touched in 2023 is a liability for AEO; it signals staleness to every system that queries it.

What does structured data add?

Schema.org markup gives AI systems explicit, machine-readable information about what your business is, what it does, and where it operates; rather than requiring inference from page text. LocalBusiness schema with areaServed, hasOfferCatalog, and review properties is the baseline for any Alberta service business pursuing AEO.
