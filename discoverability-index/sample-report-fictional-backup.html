03

What we found

Invisible across AI answers

Across 16 answers — covering ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity for the questions local patients type — your clinic is never named. Competitors appear in two to five each. In the AI-mediated version of this market, the clinic does not exist.

Why it matters: more patients begin here every month.

The website sends almost no relevance signal

The most important pages are missing the basic elements search engines rely on to understand and rank a clinic. The site reads as "generic dental page" rather than "the dentist patients in this neighbourhood are searching for."

Fix horizon: quick wins, measurable within 30 days.

Not machine-readable to AI agents

The clinic's core facts — what it offers, where it is, when it's open — live only in page text, not in a form AI tools and assistants can reliably read and cite. This is the layer competitors are starting to win on.

Fix horizon: foundational, high impact.

A strong reputation that isn't reaching the systems

The clinic's reviews are positive but thin in volume and recency on the Canadian platforms AI engines treat as trust signals — so an existing strength isn't translating into discoverability.

Fix horizon: ongoing, compounding.

Genuine longevity and patient loyalty

The clinic has the kind of track record these systems reward — it's simply buried where engines and new patients can't see it. Surfacing it is one of the fastest, highest-impact moves available.

An asset to build on.
04

The competitive field

Two clinics are winning two different games — and your clinic is in neither. Westhollow Dental leads AI search: not because it's the biggest, but because it made its credentials easy for these tools to read. Kingsmere Dental wins classic search on the strength of review volume and content depth.

The legacy peers tell the most useful story: clinics no larger or older than yours clear the mid-40s on the index through review base and Canadian recognition alone. Your clinic's own longevity story isn't reaching the systems that would surface it.
