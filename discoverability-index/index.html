Questions

Straight answers

How is this different from a free AI visibility checker?

A free grader gives you one number from an AI model's memory. The Discoverability Index runs live queries across five AI engines, scores you against seven to ten named local competitors, checks what AI gets wrong about your business, verifies your website's structured data by live browser render, models the revenue at stake, and gives you a prioritized plan — and we can do the fixes.

Do you just hand me a report, or do you fix it?

Both are available. The Report is the diagnosis. The Fix Sprint adds the done-for-you work — structured data, llms.txt, meta and headings, Google Business Profile — so the gaps actually close, not just get named.

Is this relevant to my industry?

It fits any local business buyers research before they call — trades, home services, dental and health clinics, professional services, specialty retail. If customers ask around (or ask AI) before choosing, discoverability decides who they find.

How long does it take?

A Report is scoped for delivery within about a week. Most quick-win fixes land within a single search-cycle — weeks, not months.

What does it cost to start?

The Discoverability Report is $1,500 CAD, and it is credited in full against the Fix Sprint. If you go ahead with the fixes, the Report costs you nothing. One recovered job usually covers it either way.
